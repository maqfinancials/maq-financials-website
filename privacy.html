Privacy notice

This working notice describes the website contact form. It is not a final legal privacy policy and should be reviewed for the full business and website practices.

This notice reflects the current contact form setup. Before relying on it as a complete policy, confirm the legal business entity, hosting and any analytics or cookie practices, data retention, and applicable legal obligations.

Information collected

If you submit the contact form, it collects your name and business email, plus any business type, service interest, and message you choose to provide. Please do not submit account numbers, tax IDs, passwords, financial statements, or other sensitive financial information.

How the form is handled

The form sends your submission to FormSubmit, a third-party form processing service, which forwards it to MAQfinancials at consulting@talhaqayyum.com. FormSubmit says it retains submissions for up to 30 days. See FormSubmit’s privacy information for its own practices. The message may also remain in the business email inbox according to that mailbox’s settings.

The information is used to respond to your inquiry and discuss possible services. No files can be attached through this form.

Privacy requests

For questions or requests about an inquiry you submitted, contact consulting@talhaqayyum.com.

Other website practices

This notice covers the contact form only. Update it if analytics, advertising pixels, cookies, or additional data collection are added to the website.
