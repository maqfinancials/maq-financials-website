MAQ Financials ("we", "us") provides bookkeeping services to U.S. businesses. This policy explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you fill in our questionnaire we collect your first name, company name, email, phone number, your answers about your business, and whether you agreed to text reminders. We also collect basic usage data (pages visited, the ad or link that brought you here) through cookies and the Meta Pixel.

How we use it

To prepare for and schedule your Books Review, to contact you about it, to send reminders you asked for, and to measure which ads and pages work. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Service providers that help us run this site and our business, such as our scheduling tool, CRM / form provider, email and SMS provider, and Meta (Facebook) for ad measurement. They may only use it to provide services to us.

Text messages

If you opt in, we send appointment reminders by SMS. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time or HELP for help. Opt-in data is never shared with third parties for their marketing.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing consulting@talhaqayyum.com. You can block cookies in your browser and manage ad preferences in your Facebook settings.

Accounting data

We never ask for your accounting software password. Access to your books is granted by you through your software's accountant-invite feature and can be removed by you at any time.
