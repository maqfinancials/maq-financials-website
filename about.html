About MAQfinancials

Bookkeeping should make the picture clearer.

MAQfinancials provides practical bookkeeping support for U.S. small businesses. We focus on clear expectations, organized records, and straightforward communication.

Our approach

Clear expectations from the start.

Every business has a different recordkeeping situation. A useful working relationship begins with understanding the current records, the support needed, and the decisions the information should help inform.

MAQfinancials will confirm the specific service scope, delivery schedule, fees, and responsibilities with each client before work begins.

About the founder

A decade of financial experience, focused on clarity.

Mian Talha Qayyum brings about a decade of experience across financial analysis, reporting, forecasting, and business operations. He holds an MSc in Business Analysis and Consulting with Distinction from the University of Strathclyde and a BSc (Hons) in Accounting and Finance from LSE.

A QuickBooks certified professional, Talha is also trained in Sage Intacct, Deltek Vantagepoint, Fathom reporting, and Xero. He applies this experience through MAQfinancials’ practical approach: clear scope, organized records, and straightforward communication for small businesses.
